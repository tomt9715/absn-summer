Neuro Assessment / Neurologic Dysfunction

CSF & lumbar puncture

Normal CSF is clearAnything not clear = abnormal. Cloudy in a febrile patient → suspect bacterial meningitis, anticipate antibiotics.
  • CSF analysis diagnoses: infections (meningitis, encephalitis), autoimmune disorders (GBS, MS — look for elevated protein), bleeding, tumors
  • LP = needle into subarachnoid space, usually lower lumbar level
  • Nurse role: comfort during procedure; after, keep site clean, monitor for infection
  • Post-dural puncture headache — positional, worse upright/better lying flat, usually self-limiting/managed conservatively (fluids, caffeine, lying flat) — different from a true infection (redness/warmth/purulent drainage = report)

Health history

Ask about: pain, seizures (duration, precipitating factors), dizziness vs vertigo, visual disturbances, weakness (which muscle groups), abnormal sensations (fall/injury risk), past/family/social history.

Dizziness vs. vertigoDizziness = abnormal sensation of imbalance/movement. Vertigo = illusion of movement, usually rotational.

5 components of the neuro physical exam

  1. Consciousness and cognition
  2. Motor system — motor ability and muscle strength
  3. Balance and coordination — Romberg test
  4. Sensory system — light touch, pain, temperature, vibration
  5. Reflexes
RombergPositive (sway/lose balance with eyes closed, feet together) = loss of proprioception/vestibular function, not a cerebellar or strength issue.

Geriatric neuro assessment

Don't blame aging without investigationDiminished strength/agility can be normal aging. Localized weakness can only be attributed to disease — always investigate.
  • Normal aging: gradual symmetric strength decline, slowed reaction time, diminished vibration sense (bilateral, distal)
  • NOT normal aging: sudden, unilateral weakness/numbness — red flag for stroke
  • Fall risk must be evaluated and fall-prevention measures instituted — this is the priority action when deficits are found
  • Teaching: unrushed pace, short/concrete/reinforced, large print + high-contrast + glare-free lighting for visual changes, account for possible impaired hearing and slowed responses

Eye and Vision Disorders

The 3 big ones — know how to tell them apart

ConditionVision loss patternKey clue
GlaucomaPeripheral first → tunnel vision↑IOP (normal 10-21), often asymptomatic early — "silent thief of sight"
Macular degeneration (AMD)Central vision, peripheral preservedWavy lines on Amsler grid = report immediately
Retinal detachmentCurtain/shade over part of visionPainless but a surgical EMERGENCY — floaters + flashes, caused by trauma
CataractsProgressive cloudy/blurryGlare, halos, faded colors, brunescence (lens yellows/browns)

Glaucoma

Aqueous humor buildup → ↑IOP → optic nerve damage → irreversible loss. Acute attack: severe pain, fixed mid-dilated pupil, N/V, halos — surgical emergency, call provider now.

Lifelong dropsNo cure, just IOP control — teach even when asymptomatic. Avoid activities that raise IOP: straining, tight collars, bending at the waist, Valsalva.

Cataracts

  • Pre-op: dilating eye drops as ordered, pick up post-op drops from pharmacy before surgery
  • Post-op: eye shield at night, glasses/sunglasses during day, no bending/straining/lifting/rubbing, written AND verbal instructions
  • Mild discomfort expected (Tylenol); call immediately for vision changes, floaters/flashes, increasing redness/swelling/pain, purulent drainage
  • Forceful sneezing raises IOP — can disrupt the repair

Retinal detachment

Caused by trauma. Painless "curtain/shade" + floaters + flashes = surgical emergency. Pre-op: supine, head immobilized. Post-op: prone (pushes pressure to front of eye), restrict head/eye movement.

AMD

Macula deterioration → central vision loss, peripheral preserved. Wavy Amsler grid lines = report, may signal progression to wet AMD. Verteporfin photodynamic therapy → avoid sunlight/bright light for 5 days post-procedure.

Eye drop administration & conjunctivitis

  1. Wash hands, position semi-Fowler's, remove contact lenses first
  2. Tilt head back, pull down lower lid, instill into conjunctival sac — never touch eye/lashes with dropper
  3. Pressure at the puncta (inner canthus) — prevents systemic absorption via nasolacrimal duct
  4. Wait 5 min between different drops; drops before ointment
Conjunctivitis (pink eye)Very contagious — hand hygiene is the primary nursing intervention to prevent spread.

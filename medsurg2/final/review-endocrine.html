Endocrine Disorders

Pituitary

  • Panhypopituitarism — total gland destruction → extreme weight loss, atrophy of endocrine organs, hypometabolism, hypoglycemia → coma/death if hormones not replaced
  • Acromegaly — GH excess AFTER puberty (growth plates closed) → bone/soft tissue overgrowth
  • Gigantism — GH excess BEFORE growth plate closure (childhood) → proportional overgrowth
  • Dwarfism — GH deficiency in childhood → disproportionate head/extremities to torso, bowed legs
  • Cabergoline PO for microadenomas

Shared symptoms: fatigue, insomnia, hair loss. Graves disease = autoimmune, affects women 8x more than men.

LevothyroxineTake in the morning, empty stomach. Watch for overdose signs: palpitations, insomnia, tremors, increased appetite.

Thyroidectomy

  • Post-op: semi-Fowler's, support head/neck, assess voice (discourage talking), monitor posterior dressing for bleeding
  • Calcium gluconate at bedside for ANY thyroid surgery — parathyroids sit on the thyroid, risk of accidental injury → hypocalcemia within 24-48 hrs → tetany/laryngospasm/airway compromise
  • Watch Chvostek's + Trousseau's signs

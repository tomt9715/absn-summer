GI disorder diets

GERDAvoid foods that lower LES pressure: fatty foods, caffeine, chocolate, alcohol, peppermint/spearmint; don't eat 3 hrs before lying down
Gastritis/PUDAvoid ETOH, NSAIDs, frequent small meals (↑ acid secretion), coffee, caffeine, spicy foods
Lactose intoleranceSmall amounts (4-6oz milk) tolerated; avoid high-lactose foods

IBSLow FODMAP — limits high-fructose/short-chain carbs (e.g. raisins)

Dumping syndromeEarly (10-20 min): fullness/cramping, N/V, diarrhea, vasomotor (faintness, diaphoresis, tachycardia). Late (1-3 hrs): anxiety, diaphoresis, hunger — from a rapid glucose rise + insulin surge causing rebound hypoglycemia. Manage: small frequent meals w/ protein+fat, liquids 1 hr AFTER meals not with, lie down after eating.

Dysphagia diet

Upright/high Fowler's position to facilitate swallowing. Pills with ≥8oz fluid (can be thickened). Avoid thin liquids and sticky food. Levels: pureed → mechanically altered → advanced; liquids: thin → nectar → honey → spoon-thick.

AKI — 3 phases, diet changes

Oliguric → diuretic → recovery. Can cause hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, hypocalcemia, hyperphosphatemia, fluid overload. Diet: simple carbs, fats, low-protein starches, possible Na/K restriction. Fluids restricted in oliguric phase, increased in diuretic phase.

Cultural & religious considerations

  • Hispanic/Latino — "hot"/"cold" food balance beliefs; decreased whole grain/milk intake; high obesity/T2DM prevalence
  • Asian American — yin (seaweed, fruit/veg, cold beverages) / yang (fried, coffee, spice, meat) balance; lowest obesity prevalence but higher T2DM risk with increased body fat
  • Religious: Kosher/Halal = no blood consumed; Orthodox Judaism doesn't mix meat+dairy; Islam/Hindu/Mormon/SDA avoid alcohol; pork avoided in Judaism/Islam/Hindu/Buddhism

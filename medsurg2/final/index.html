How to get the most out of this

  • →This exam is cumulative — if a chapter above feels shaky, go back to that exam's hub for the full Knowledge Check and Deep Drill banks before running the simulator.
  • →The 6 EKG strips on the real exam are their own skill — practice those separately with the EKG Interpreter on the Exam 1 hub. This simulator tests rhythm recognition and next-action through clinical scenarios instead of strip images.
  • →Questions and answer choices reshuffle every time — so you're learning the material, not memorizing where the answer sits.
  • →Retake as many times as you want — your best score is saved.
